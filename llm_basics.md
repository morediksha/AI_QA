Trained on vast amounts of data—such as books, articles, and websites—these models learn language patterns to answer questions, write code, summarize documents, and translate languages

### **Ollama**

[**Ollama**](https://ollama.com/) is **a free, open-source application that allows you to download and run Large Language Models (LLMs) locally directly on your own computer**. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UtSSMs6ObqY), [2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5RIOQuHOihY&t=15)]Normally, when you use a chatbot like ChatGPT, your prompts are sent over the internet to massive cloud data centers. Ollama bypasses the cloud entirely, acting like a "package manager" for AI that lets you use your own computer's graphics card (GPU) and processor (CPU) to power the AI. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZPZw_A6zlZ8), [2](https://www.youstable.com/blog/ollama-vs-openai), [3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5RIOQuHOihY&t=15), [4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NwPv6IyfitQ&t=89)]**Why People Use Ollama**
• **100% Data Privacy:** Because the model runs entirely on your physical machine, your text, code, and private documents are never sent to external servers or used to train public models.
• **Zero Cost:** Ollama is completely free. You do not have to pay monthly subscriptions or API usage fees to query the models.
• **Offline Access:** Once you download a model to your hard drive, you can use the AI without an active internet connection.
• **Developer Friendly:** Ollama automatically sets up a local background server (on `localhost:11434`), allowing developers to easily connect the AI into their own software programs, coding assistants, or private apps
